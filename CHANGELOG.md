# Changelog

Every TestFlight build of Mneme, newest first. The marketing version is **1.0**; builds are
numbered. Builds 1 to 9 were the Jellyfin app this one replaces. Each entry lists what a viewer
would notice, not every commit.

## Build 10

### Changed
- **Mneme is now the app for Melete.** It signs in to a Melete server (Quick Connect, or a
  password) instead of Jellyfin. Everyone signs in again once.
- Artwork, AI search and requests come from Melete: nothing to set up on the Apple TV.
- Requests are Melete's own, from Search, and show how each is getting on as it downloads.

### Added
- Play says what it will do: "Resume S2:E4 · 23m left", "Play S3:E1", "Play Again".
- A click on the remote's edge jumps ten seconds; a swipe scrubs over seek-bar previews.
- Skip Intro and Next Episode are buttons that take focus when they appear.
- Find by Description on Search, when the server has AI on.
- Home and My List follow changes made elsewhere as they happen.

### Not yet back
- Trailers in the hero, and Featured for You.
- Cast, extras and collections on a title's page.
- The iPhone and iPad app.
