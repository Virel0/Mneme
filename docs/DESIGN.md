# Mneme design

Mneme is the Apple TV app for [Melete](https://github.com/Virel0/Melete). This file records the
decisions behind it and the reasons, newest last. A decision changes by adding a row that
replaces it, not by editing the old one.

| # | Decision | Because | Date |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Mneme talks only to Melete, through its documented API (`/api/v1`, contract in Melete's `src/api/openapi.json`). No Jellyfin support | One server to follow means one set of types and no compatibility layer; Melete's API was written with this app as a client | 2026-10-30 |
| 2 | Built new rather than adapted from Swiftfin, the Jellyfin app the previous Mneme was a fork of. Mneme's own screens (hero, tile rows, the focused title's spotlight, tab strip) are carried across and rebuilt on Melete's types | About four fifths of the fork was Swiftfin's Jellyfin plumbing, and Melete answers most of what that plumbing assembled (`/home` is the whole home in one request) | 2026-10-30 |
| 3 | Apple TV first. iOS and iPadOS come after the Apple TV app replaces the Jellyfin one | The television is where the app is used; the fork's iOS app was Swiftfin's, unchanged | 2026-10-30 |
| 4 | The API client is its own package, `MeleteKit`, with hand-written models. Its tests read `src/api/openapi.json` from a Melete checkout beside this repository (or `MELETE_OPENAPI`), build a full value from each schema, decode it, encode it again and require the same keys back, so a model that drifts from the contract fails `swift test` | Hand-written models stay readable and small; a generator would add a dependency for the same guarantee. Melete keeps its contract the same way | 2026-10-30 |
| 5 | The Xcode project uses synchronized folders: files under `Mneme/` belong to the app without being listed in the project file | A project file that lists nothing rarely conflicts, and needs no generator tool | 2026-10-30 |
| 6 | Signing in is Quick Connect first, a password second. Each person who signs in on the device keeps a session: the session in user defaults, its token only in the keychain | Typing a password with a remote is the worst part of a TV app. Melete issues a token per device, so profiles on one Apple TV are separate sessions | 2026-10-30 |
| 7 | Mneme stays under the Mozilla Public License 2.0 | Files carried across from the fork were published under it, and it sits well beside Melete's GPL | 2026-10-30 |
| 8 | The bundle identifier and build numbers carry on from the Jellyfin app (first build 10) | TestFlight testers get the new app as an update, not a second app | 2026-10-30 |
| 9 | Shows, Anime and Movies are the home narrowed to their kind, as Melete's web pages narrow it: a movie is a movie, and a series is anime when `/series?anime=only` lists it. Each page then lists every title of its kind A to Z, a page of 100 at a time | One request answers every Watch page's hero and rows; the anime list is the server's own rule for what anime is, so the app and the web never disagree | 2026-10-30 |
| 10 | Play says what it will do, worked out as Melete's web player does: the episode the home names as next or partway through, else the movie's progress, else the first episode on disk, Specials last | The same title must not offer "Resume" in one place and "Play S1:E1" in another | 2026-10-30 |
| 11 | A tile wears one badge: "N Unwatched" only on a series being watched (in Continue Watching or Next Up), else "New" for 14 days after a title came in, unless watched. Posters show only the number | Every series has episodes nobody has watched, so the count means something only for one in progress; a poster has room for the number and no more | 2026-10-30 |
| 12 | Tiles lift and the title they rest on is shown above the rows (the spotlight); rows rest just under it, 180 points clear of the bottom of the screen; the page fades out above the focused row. Carried across from the fork with its timings | tvOS scrolls a focused view nearer the edge than that, so resting there leaves it nothing to undo; the timings are what kept the spotlight from flashing between tiles | 2026-10-30 |
| 13 | Where a feature waits on Melete (recommendations for "Featured for You", trailers for preview clips, a per-user auto-play setting), debug builds show a marker in its place and release builds show nothing | Testers see a finished page; developers see what is still owed by the server | 2026-10-30 |
| 14 | Playback is AetherEngine (FFmpeg demuxing into VideoToolbox) on the file Melete's `play` links to, pinned to 7.42.0, the newest release that builds with Xcode 26.6. The audio Melete picks for the viewer is opened from the first frame; its subtitle pick is applied once the file is open: an embedded track through the engine, any other as WebVTT from the stream link | Direct play keeps every track local, so changing audio is a call into the engine rather than a new stream; Melete already knows the viewer's languages and the series' last choice | 2026-10-30 |
| 15 | Progress goes to Melete every 10 seconds while playing, on pause and on leaving, with `restart` on every report of a Play Again | Melete marks a file watched at 90% and ignores a report near the start that would undo a resume, unless told the viewer started over | 2026-10-30 |
| 16 | Recaps, intros and previews are offered as Skip while they play, or skipped on their own when the device setting says so; credits never are. The next episode counts down from 10 at the credits (or 20 seconds from the end without them); Select plays it, Menu turns it away | Skipping credits ends the episode, which is the next-episode offer's job; one press either way keeps the remote simple | 2026-10-30 |
| 17 | Auto-play and auto-skip are settings of the Apple TV, not of the person | Melete keeps no such setting per user yet; when it does, they move there | 2026-10-30 |
| 18 | Diagnostics are the last 250 lines of what playback did (each stall with the engine's measurements, and the measurements every 10 seconds while playing), sent from Settings to `POST /api/v1/me/diagnostics` | A report is sent after the fact; the run-up to a stall is what explains it. Melete refuses a body over 64 KiB, which 250 lines of 200 characters stays under | 2026-10-30 |
| 19 | Search finds titles in the library as the viewer types, and on TMDB through Melete for the rest, which can be requested from there. Before typing it shows the viewer's requests and, when Melete has discovery on, what is trending. With AI on in Melete, Find by Description asks for movies and shows matching the words | One field for finding, asking for and describing; the server decides what is available, so the app shows each part only when it answers | 2026-10-30 |
| 20 | Top Shelf shows Continue Watching and Next Up (wide, playing) and Latest Movies and Shows (posters, opening the title), written by the app after each home load into the shared app group, pictures saved as files there; `mneme://` links open a title or play a file. The bundle identifiers and app group are the Jellyfin app's | The extension cannot sign in and Melete's artwork needs the session's token; reusing the identifiers keeps TestFlight's existing app record and provisioning | 2026-10-30 |
| 21 | A file over the way in's bitrate limit, or the limit chosen in Settings, plays through Melete's conversion: HEVC where the Apple TV decodes it in hardware, else H.264. Subtitles still come from the stream link | Melete refuses a stream over a way in's limit, so a viewer outside the house gets the conversion instead of an error; HEVC keeps HDR where it can | 2026-10-30 |
| 22 | The hero and the spotlight play a quiet clip of the title itself, through Melete's conversion at 720p and 3 Mbit/s, from where it was left or an eighth of the way in, only on the home network | A clip of the film says more than a still and needs no trailer; away from home the bandwidth belongs to what is being watched | 2026-10-30 |
| 23 | The home is kept on the device per person and drawn at launch while Melete is asked again; a failed refresh leaves it on screen | The first screen should not wait on the network, and an error should not replace a page that was fine | 2026-10-30 |
| 24 | An 8-second rendered intro (`Mneme/Resources/Intro.mp4`, 4K HEVC at 8 Mbit/s) plays once per launch over the app, which loads behind it; any press skips it, and with Reduce Motion on the wordmark shows still for a moment instead | A rendered clip can carry picture and sound that a drawn animation cannot, and nobody should wait on branding to watch something; 8 Mbit/s is the lowest rate that kept the clip's specks of light from smearing | 2026-10-30 |
| 25 | A pressed tile's picture grows from where it sat to the whole screen while the title's page fades in beneath; the backdrop on a title's page leans in 7% and back over a minute | tvOS crossfades between pages and does not draw SwiftUI's zoom transition, so the zoom is drawn over the crossfade; the drift keeps a page being read from feeling still. Both stop with Reduce Motion | 2026-10-30 |
| 26 | Playback starts from black: the page fades out, the player goes in without a slide, and the picture fades up when it first plays | A slide into a black player read as a glitch; a fade is how a film starts | 2026-10-30 |
| 27 | Loading is shown with the wordmark's rule and diamond (`LoadingMark`) on whole pages and in the player; small waits inside buttons and Settings keep the system spinner | The mark is the app's own; a form row is the system's | 2026-10-30 |
| 28 | Pages with nothing else to show (My List empty, a load that failed, a file that cannot be played) and the sign-in screen are given over to one of the app's pictures, Greek vase painting redrawn as the wordmark's neon line, with the words on the picture's dark left side over a fade (`ArtPage`, `ArtBackdrop`) | An empty or failed page is the one moment nothing else competes for the screen; one figure per page (Calliope, Hermes, Melpomene, Mneme) says what happened in the app's own voice | 2026-10-30 |
| 29 | "No matches" in Search is a strip of Urania, the Muse of astronomy, that fades into the page on every side | The search keyboard holds the top of the screen, so the picture fits the space beneath it; the page keeps margins a hard-edged strip would stop at | 2026-10-30 |
| 30 | "Who's Watching?" has a silent 8-second loop of drifting light behind it, at 60%, 1080p HEVC with no audio track; nothing with Reduce Motion on | Movement on the first screen without sound that could interrupt anything; the clip was made to loop by dissolving its last two seconds into its first | 2026-10-30 |
| 31 | The Top Shelf image, shown when the app has nothing of its own to list, is the nine Muses in procession with the wordmark between them; JPEG, the marketing sizes sharing the same files | PNG made the four sizes 42 MB for a picture JPEG holds in 3 MB | 2026-10-30 |
| 32 | The page colour is the violet black the app's pictures are drawn on (0.04, 0.016, 0.08), and the launch screen with it | A neutral near-black beside the pictures read as grey on a television, which lifts near-blacks, in every fade from a picture into the page | 2026-10-30 |
| 33 | The spotlight's backdrop fills the screen and darkens towards the foot instead of stopping above the lower rows; pictures behind pages are laid out over a view that takes the space it is given | A fixed-height backdrop left the lower rows on bare page; a picture sized directly asked for more than the screen and pushed the tab strip off the top | 2026-10-30 |
| 34 | Tabs crossfade in 0.3 s, and the app comes up out of the intro over 1 s, settling from 4% larger | Pages that cut from one to the next, and an intro that ended in a cut, read as abrupt | 2026-10-30 |
| 35 | The hero plays a pick's trailer when Melete has one in the title's folder, muted, through the same 720p conversion as the clips, unless the viewer set hero trailers off in Melete; otherwise the clip of the title itself | Melete's online trailers are YouTube's web player, which an Apple TV app cannot show | 2026-10-30 |
| 36 | "No matches" puts Urania behind the whole Search page, lowered to start beneath the keyboard; replaces 29 | The strip took too little of the screen | 2026-10-30 |
| 37 | A password sign-in that Melete answers with a two-factor challenge asks for the authenticator's six-digit code and finishes with it; a challenge that ran out (five minutes) goes back to the password | Melete's two-factor sign-in; Quick Connect is unaffected, as it is approved from a session already signed in | 2026-10-31 |
| 38 | With "Melete converts surround sound" on (off by default), a soundtrack the Apple TV can't play itself (TrueHD, DTS, PCM) is played through Melete's copy: the video unchanged, the audio converted there, lossless as FLAC, Dolby Vision kept where the screen shows it. A refusal plays the file as it is | Takes the converting off the Apple TV, at the cost of TrueHD's Atmos; off by default because that cost is the viewer's to choose, and a server before copy kept lossless would send AAC and HDR10 | 2026-10-31 |
| 39 | Auto play of the next episode and its countdown come from Melete per person, and Settings changes them there; the device's setting only for a server that doesn't keep them | Melete keeps them for every device the viewer uses | 2026-10-31 |
| 40 | The home shows Melete's Because You Watched rows (titled with each seed) and Featured for You; Mneme's own Because You Watched only for a server that sends none. A title's page has More Like This, the titles in the library like it | Melete makes these now, the AI's picks included, so the app need not | 2026-10-31 |
| 41 | A request opens its own page: what was asked for, who decided and when (and why, for a decline), and once approved the steps from waiting to in the library with the one it is on, the download's percent or the copy home, Melete's latest word, the source and when it last changed, and a series season by season. A request still waiting can be taken back or its season changed; a series can be asked for one season | Melete reports all of this to the requester, and the live list keeps the page moving; a season is a number Melete checks, as it doesn't know a series' seasons before it is in the library | 2026-11-02 |
| 42 | Every step of a preview clip or hero trailer that can fail writes a line to the diagnostics report | Clips failed without a trace | 2026-11-02 |
| 43 | Pages sit on `PageBackground`: the page colour, a soft violet light rising from below, a scatter of faint stars thinning towards the top, and Cassiopeia and Lyra (the Muses' lyre) in the top corners beside the tab strip. "Who's Watching?" keeps its loop and sign-in its picture | A flat colour looked unfinished beside the pictures; the stars and constellations are theirs. Nothing near the top where pictures fade into the page, so no edge shows | 2026-11-02 |
| 44 | `PageBackground` is a night sky picture (faint stars, constellations in the corners, a violet and cyan haze rising from below), its colours shifted so its dark top is exactly the page colour, with a shooting star drawn in code every 15 to 40 seconds at a random place in the upper sky; none with Reduce Motion. Replaces the drawn stars of 43 | The picture is richer than drawn stars; a shooting star drawn in code comes at its own moment and place each time, where a looping video would repeat itself, and costs neither file size nor decoding | 2026-11-02 |
| 45 | The sky has depth: when focus moves, it eases up to 2% of focus's distance from the screen's centre the other way (about 19 points across, 11 up or down), drawn 4% larger so no edge shows. It follows the hour in its lower part only: dawn amber 5 to 9, a cooler blue by day to 17, rose dusk to 21, plain night after. Neither moves with Reduce Motion | Something far behind moves less, and the other way, than what is near; a sky that matches the time of day is part of the room. The top stays the page colour for the fades | 2026-11-02 |
| 46 | Preview clips and hero trailers play the file itself through its stream link, as the player does, with the television left in its mode (the engine's `displayCriteriaOwner = .host`), and their sound faded up to 60% unless Settings turns it off; replaces the 720p, muted conversion of 22 and 35 | The conversion looked soft and silent on a 4K television; on the home network the file itself starts as fast, costs Melete nothing, and keeping the display mode stops an HDR clip blanking the screen | 2026-11-02 |
| 47 | Mneme is under the GNU General Public License, version 3 or later; replaces 7 | The same licence as Melete and its other apps. The files carried across from the fork were Mneme's own, not Swiftfin's, and no file carries another project's copyright; the dependencies (AetherEngine under LGPL 3 with an app store exception, FFmpeg under LGPL 2.1, Nuke and LibDovi under MIT) all go with it | 2026-11-02 |
| 48 | The focused title's colour (its backdrop's average, lifted to glow, worked out on the Apple TV and kept per picture) lights the sky, glows up from under the rows on Home, and haloes the focused tile and the hero card | The screen follows the title instead of being a fixed dark frame with pictures on it; only the focused tile's colour is worked out, so a scroll costs nothing | 2026-11-02 |
| 49 | After 45 seconds without a press, swipe or focus move on Home, with nothing open over it, the hero's picks take the screen one at a time with their trailer or clip, changing every 40 seconds; any press, Menu included, brings Home back with focus on the tab strip. After eight it rests on a still | The television on and alive; the page under it is switched off so the waking press does nothing else, and stopping lets the Apple TV's screensaver come | 2026-11-02 |
| 50 | A title's page plays its trailer (an extra of kind trailer) or a clip of it behind the header, while the header is in view and the page on screen | The page opens on the film moving, as the hero does; it stops once the episodes are being read | 2026-11-02 |
| 51 | The hero and the spotlight drift slowly, as a title's page does, while their clip loads or when clips are off | A still that sits a while looks dead | 2026-11-02 |
| 52 | Top Shelf is the hero's picks as a full-width carousel (each with its overview and facts, Play to start, Select to open), replacing Continue Watching and the rows; a pick with a trailer in its folder plays a muted preview of it, through Melete's HLS copy of the trailer, while preview clips and Melete's hero trailers are on. A link is used again for 20 hours | tvOS gives an app one Top Shelf style; the carousel shows the films moving, as Apple's own apps do. The system plays a preview from its start, so only trailers get one; a link costs Melete nothing until it plays, and lasts 24 hours | 2026-11-02 |
| 53 | A title's page plays its trailer behind the header only when it is at least 1080 lines tall, or as tall as the title's own tallest file where that is lower (a trailer not measured yet counts); otherwise a clip of the title, the file itself. Refines 50 | Melete's rule for the hero's trailers: a soft trailer behind a sharp film looks worse than the film | 2026-11-02 |
| 54 | The pressed tile's picture turns into the page's own backdrop as it grows, and fades only once it fills the screen as that backdrop. Refines 25 | Fading the tile's picture itself showed a poster stretched to the screen through the page as it came in, a flash of different art before the page | 2026-11-02 |
| 55 | The tabs run Home, Movies, Shows, Anime, My List, Requests, Search. Requests lists the viewer's film and series requests in rows by where each has got to (on the way, waiting for approval, in the library, declined), each opening its request page; music asked for elsewhere is left out | Requests made from Search had nowhere of their own to be followed; Mneme plays no music, and music requests have no poster | 2026-11-02 |
| 56 | Each Top Shelf picture is drawn by the app: the title's backdrop with its logo (or name), facts and overview in the upper left, as the idle screen shows them. The carousel item still carries its title, summary and details. Refines 52 | tvOS 26 draws only a carousel item's picture, arrows and page dots, none of its title, summary or details, even full screen. Upper left keeps the words clear of the clock, of the arrow halfway down the left edge, and of the row of apps that covers the lower part until the shelf is opened | 2026-11-02 |
| 57 | AetherEngine comes straight from its own repository, pinned to the exact release 7.54.0. Refines 14 | The fork it came from carried no changes of its own and had stopped following upstream; 7.54.0 builds with Xcode 26.6 for the simulator and the device | 2026-11-02 |
| 58 | Shows, Anime and Movies each ask Melete for their own home (`/home?kind=`) and show its rows in its order, "Because You Watched" included; until a page's own home comes, and from a server that ignores `kind`, the page shows the whole home narrowed to its kind, leaving out a Because You Watched row whose title is of another kind. New Episodes is a row of what is being watched, as Continue Watching and Next Up are: wide tiles, and the episode a title's Play starts. Rows Melete adds are titled as its web pages title them (Next in the Collection, Highly Rated, Not Seen Yet, Watch Again). Refines 9 | Melete gives each page its own hero and rows, so a page has its own Because You Watched instead of four rows; New Episodes takes shows out of Next Up, and without it they would lose their Play episode and wide tiles | 2026-11-02 |
| 59 | A person's Melete profile picture stands in for their initial on the profile button and on Who's Watching and Profiles, each fetched as that person since a profile can be on another server; the initial shows while it loads and without one. The signed-in user is read again from `/me` each time the app opens | Melete gives everyone a profile picture that every app shows beside their name; the user kept from signing in predates it and would never show a new one | 2026-11-02 |
| 60 | ASS/SSA subtitles will be drawn with libass. It is built from source by `Packages/Libass/build.sh`: libass 0.17.5, FreeType 2.15.0, HarfBuzz 15.0.0 and FriBidi 1.0.18, each checked against a pinned SHA-256 (FreeType's signature checked too), compiled with Xcode's clang alone, to a reproducible `Libass.xcframework` recorded in `BUILD-INFO.txt`. Fonts come through CoreText and from the file itself; there is no Fontconfig, libpng or iconv, and FreeType has only its TrueType, CFF and SFNT drivers. AetherEngine needs no change: `preserveASSMarkup`, `fontAttachments` and `ASSScriptBuilder` give the renderer what it needs (issue #17) | swift-ass-renderer, the renderer AetherEngine's docs name, bundles prebuilt 2024 binaries that can't be tied to source, with outdated libraries; these libraries parse subtitles and fonts out of media files. Its Swift layer (MIT) remains the model for the renderer | 2026-11-02 |
| 61 | A track Melete marks `ass`, with an `ass_url`, is drawn by Mneme with libass: the script from `ass_url` (decoded as its charset says) and the video's fonts from the stream link's `fonts` (up to 96 MB together), laid over the picture where the video sits, on the engine's clock, about 30 times a second and only when libass says something changed. Until it is ready, and when anything fails, the track's plain version shows as before; the report says which. The four libraries' licences are under Settings → Acknowledgements, with FreeType's credit. Telling Melete the player draws ASS waits on Melete #162. Refines 60 | Signs, fonts, positions and karaoke are what anime subtitles are made of; the WebVTT version keeps only the words | 2026-11-04 |
| 62 | iPhone and iPad are the same app target as the Apple TV, under the same bundle identifier: one Mneme scheme builds for tvOS and iOS. Code only the Apple TV has (Top Shelf, Match Content) is left out of the iOS build per file or with `#if os(tvOS)`; the remote's buttons and focus sections do nothing there (`RemoteOnly.swift`) until each screen has a touch layout. libass is built for iOS too. Replaces 3 | Most of the app (MeleteKit, sessions, the library, playback, subtitles) is the same on both; one target and one identifier make one App Store app, one build number sequence and one CI | 2026-11-06 |
