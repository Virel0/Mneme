# Mneme design

Mneme is the Apple TV app for [Melete](https://github.com/Virel0/Melete). This file records the
decisions behind it and the reasons, newest last. A decision changes by adding a row that
replaces it, not by editing the old one.

| # | Decision | Because | Date |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Mneme talks only to Melete, through its documented API (`/api/v1`, contract in Melete's `src/api/openapi.json`). No Jellyfin support | One server to follow means one set of types and no compatibility layer; Melete's API was written with this app as a client | 2026-10-30 |
| 2 | Built new rather than adapted from Swiftfin, the Jellyfin app the previous Mneme was a fork of. Mneme's own screens (hero, tile rows, the focused title's spotlight, tab strip) are carried across and rebuilt on Melete's types | About four fifths of the fork was Swiftfin's Jellyfin plumbing, and Melete answers most of what that plumbing assembled (`/home` is the whole home in one request) | 2026-10-30 |
| 3 | Apple TV first. iOS and iPadOS come after the Apple TV app replaces the Jellyfin one | The television is where the app is used; the fork's iOS app was Swiftfin's, unchanged | 2026-10-30 |
| 4 | The API client is its own package, `MeleteKit`, with hand-written models. Its tests read `src/api/openapi.json` from a Melete checkout beside this repository (or `MELETE_OPENAPI`), build a full value from each schema, decode it, encode it again and require the same keys back, so a model that drifts from the contract fails `swift test` | Hand-written models stay readable and small; a generator would add a dependency for the same guarantee. Melete keeps its contract the same way | 2026-10-30 |
| 5 | The Xcode project uses synchronized folders: files under `Mneme/` belong to the app without being listed in the project file | A project file that lists nothing rarely conflicts, and needs no generator tool | 2026-10-30 |
| 6 | Signing in is Quick Connect first, a password second. Each person who signs in on the device keeps a session: the session in user defaults, its token only in the keychain | Typing a password with a remote is the worst part of a TV app. Melete issues a token per device, so profiles on one Apple TV are separate sessions | 2026-10-30 |
| 7 | Mneme stays under the Mozilla Public License 2.0 | Files carried across from the fork were published under it, and it sits well beside Melete's GPL | 2026-10-30 |
| 8 | The bundle identifier and build numbers carry on from the Jellyfin app (first build 10) | TestFlight testers get the new app as an update, not a second app | 2026-10-30 |
| 9 | Shows, Anime and Movies are the home narrowed to their kind, as Melete's web pages narrow it: a movie is a movie, and a series is anime when `/series?anime=only` lists it. Each page then lists every title of its kind A to Z, a page of 100 at a time | One request answers every Watch page's hero and rows; the anime list is the server's own rule for what anime is, so the app and the web never disagree | 2026-10-30 |
| 10 | Play says what it will do, worked out as Melete's web player does: the episode the home names as next or partway through, else the movie's progress, else the first episode on disk, Specials last | The same title must not offer "Resume" in one place and "Play S1:E1" in another | 2026-10-30 |
| 11 | A tile wears one badge: "N Unwatched" only on a series being watched (in Continue Watching or Next Up), else "New" for 14 days after a title came in, unless watched. Posters show only the number | Every series has episodes nobody has watched, so the count means something only for one in progress; a poster has room for the number and no more | 2026-10-30 |
| 12 | Tiles lift and the title they rest on is shown above the rows (the spotlight); rows rest just under it, 180 points clear of the bottom of the screen; the page fades out above the focused row. Carried across from the fork with its timings | tvOS scrolls a focused view nearer the edge than that, so resting there leaves it nothing to undo; the timings are what kept the spotlight from flashing between tiles | 2026-10-30 |
| 13 | Where a feature waits on Melete (recommendations for "Featured for You", trailers for preview clips, a per-user auto-play setting), debug builds show a marker in its place and release builds show nothing | Testers see a finished page; developers see what is still owed by the server | 2026-10-30 |
| 14 | Playback is AetherEngine (FFmpeg demuxing into VideoToolbox) on the file Melete's `play` links to, pinned to 7.42.0, the newest release that builds with Xcode 26.6. The audio Melete picks for the viewer is opened from the first frame; its subtitle pick is applied once the file is open: an embedded track through the engine, any other as WebVTT from the stream link | Direct play keeps every track local, so changing audio is a call into the engine rather than a new stream; Melete already knows the viewer's languages and the series' last choice | 2026-10-30 |
| 15 | Progress goes to Melete every 10 seconds while playing, on pause and on leaving, with `restart` on every report of a Play Again | Melete marks a file watched at 90% and ignores a report near the start that would undo a resume, unless told the viewer started over | 2026-10-30 |
| 16 | Recaps, intros and previews are offered as Skip while they play, or skipped on their own when the device setting says so; credits never are. The next episode counts down from 10 at the credits (or 20 seconds from the end without them); Select plays it, Menu turns it away | Skipping credits ends the episode, which is the next-episode offer's job; one press either way keeps the remote simple | 2026-10-30 |
| 17 | Auto-play and auto-skip are settings of the Apple TV, not of the person | Melete keeps no such setting per user yet; when it does, they move there | 2026-10-30 |
| 18 | Diagnostics are the last 250 lines of what playback did (each stall with the engine's measurements, and the measurements every 10 seconds while playing), sent from Settings to `POST /api/v1/me/diagnostics` | A report is sent after the fact; the run-up to a stall is what explains it. Melete refuses a body over 64 KiB, which 250 lines of 200 characters stays under | 2026-10-30 |
